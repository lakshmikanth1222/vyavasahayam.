# Multi-Tier Rescue Engine & Solar Drying Value-Addition

## 1. Trigger Scenarios
The Rescue Engine is autonomously invoked when:
1. **Buyer Order Cancellation** after farmer harvest.
2. **Transit Delay or Logistics Disruption** (traffic blockages, vehicle breakdown).
3. **Freshness Index Decay** approaching the critical shelf-life threshold.
4. **Sudden Market Glut / Excess Local Supply**.

### Channel Priorities:
1. **Priority 1: Buyer Switching** (Instant proximity matching with nearby bulk supermarkets or commercial kitchens).
2. **Priority 2: Commercial Agro-Processing** (Sauce, puree, pulp, and pasteurization lines).
3. **Priority 3: Solar Drying Value-Addition** (Converts fresh tomatoes, chillies, mangoes, and onions into shelf-stable vacuum-sealed dried products with 10:1 yield recovery and 12-month shelf life).
4. **Priority 4: Temporary Controlled Cold Storage** (Extends quality window by 5-7 days under 0-4°C controlled atmosphere).
5. **Priority 5: Safe Farm / Cattle Feed Channel** (Permitted strictly for clean, un-mouldy, non-chemically contaminated produce).
6. **Priority 6: Waste-to-Value Biomethanation** (For spoiled organic waste).

---

## 3. Solar Drying Unit Operations
- **Centre Capacity**: 2,000 kg fresh produce / day.
- **Drying Conditions**: 50–55°C filtered hot airflow, reducing moisture from ~94% to 10–12%.
- **Output Products**:
  - *Gourmet Sun-Dried Tomato Halves (Vacuum Pack)*
  - *Guntur Red/Green Chilli Flakes*
  - *Dehydrated Rose Onion Flakes*
